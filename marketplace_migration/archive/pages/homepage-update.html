The West of England Mayoral Combined Authority

The Open Data Portal is managed by the West of England Mayoral Combined Authority. The Mayoral Combined Authority makes decisions that directly benefit our region, our residents and our economy. As a Mayoral Combined Authority, we have significant powers and funding to tackle everyday challenges in the West of England. The Mayoral Combined Authority's work covers most aspects of daily life, including homes, transport, skills, jobs and support for businesses.
